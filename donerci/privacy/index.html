Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Doner Master (called Dönerci Usta in Turkish; “the app”) collects, why, and what choices you have. The app is published by Yokbi Games, the trade name of Uğur Buğra Özkaya, an individual developer in Türkiye (“we”), who is the data controller.

In short: there is no account and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Your game progress stays on your device. The app uses Google AdMob for ads, Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous gameplay statistics, and RevenueCat to handle the one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. These services receive device-level data described below.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (day, coins, upgrades), settings (language, sound, music, haptics) and whether you bought “Remove Ads” are saved only on your device. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): on first launch the app asks for your consent to personalised ads. You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • iOS tracking: the app asks for permission to use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: buying it turns off between-day ads; rewarded ads remain optional and only play when you tap them.

4. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Personalised ads and the related use of advertising identifiers: your consent. Non-personalised ads, analytics and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding, running and improving a free game. Purchases: performance of the contract with you.

5. International transfers and retention

Google, RevenueCat and Apple may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses). Data held by these providers is kept for the periods their settings and policies define; we do not keep a separate copy.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your name or email, please include the app-related details you can share so we can help, or use the controls above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Turkish users can read the KVKK notice on the Turkish page.

8. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data use changes and change the effective date above.
