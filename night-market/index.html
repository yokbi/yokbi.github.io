Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Email yokbiyahk@gmail.com with your device model and what happened.

Bought No Ads but still see ads, or changed phones? Open Shop → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Ad privacy choices: Settings → Privacy options.

Your progress is saved only on your phone (and in its own Google or iCloud backup), so we cannot restore a lost market.
